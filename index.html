Wheel of Fortune

Enter items one per line. Click segments to exclude or restore them, keep at least 2 active items, and press Space to spin when the textarea is not focused.
Spin to pick a speaker, then click Start meeting to begin timing. Click any name during a meeting to switch the active speaker. Greyed-out names are excluded.

    Meeting summary

      Focusing the textarea stops the current spin. The current list is stored in the URL, and excluded segments stay saved locally for that wheel.
